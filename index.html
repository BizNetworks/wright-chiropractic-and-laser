Signs You May Benefit From a Visit

Nerve and joint trouble often creeps in slowly. If any of these sound familiar, an evaluation with a chiropractor is a smart next step.

  • Tingling, pins-and-needles or burning in the feet or hands
  • Numbness that makes walking or gripping harder
  • Pain that gets worse at night
  • Loose joints or frequent sprains
  • Back, neck or shoulder pain that keeps returning
  • Slow recovery after a sports injury
